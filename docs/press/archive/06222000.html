Loki Games on QLITech Advanced Multimedia Workstations

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
June 22, 2000

Loki Press Contact:
Kayt Sorhaindo
press@lokigames.com

QLITech Press Contact:
Ray Sanders
ray@qlitech.net

Tustin, CA. -- June 22, 2000 -- Loki Software, Inc., the foremost authority in bringing popular PC games to the Linux platform, today announces an agreement with QLITech Linux Computers, a leading Linux system vendor in the Midwest.

Under the terms of the agreement, QLITech now bundles a variety of hit Loki titles on the Advanced Multimedia Workstations. Customers of these high-end gaming systems now receive Civilization: Call to Power, Heavy Gear II and a Loki Games Demo CD with a full install of Eric's Ultimate Solitaire.

The Advanced Multimedia Workstations are QLITech's most popular pre-configured workstations. Featuring a Matrox Millenium G400 32Mb AGP graphics card, a 52X CD-ROM, a Creative Labs Sound Blaster Live!, with Cambridge Soundworks 5 Speaker surround sound system, the workstations offer users a premium gaming experience.

About QLITech Linux Computers

Founded in November, 1998, QLITech is a privately-held company that provides and supports custom Linux systems. QLITech is committed to providing high-end service and support at affordable rates. For more information visit http://www.qlitech.net.

About Loki Software, Inc.

Founded in 1998, CA, Loki works with leading game publishers and developers to port their best-selling PC and Macintosh titles to Linux. Loki will soon release Linux versions of SimCity 3000 Unlimited, Soldier of Fortune and Sid Meier's Alpha Centuari. For more information visit .
