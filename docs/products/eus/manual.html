We have a slight Manual Correction for Eric's Ultimate Solitaire.
When performing a manual install, you will also need to copy the icon files and the EUS.txt in order to play the game. For example:
cp /mnt/cdrom/EUS.txt /usr/local/games/EUS
cp /mnt/cdrom/icon.* /usr/local/games/EUS
cp /mnt/cdrom/README /usr/local/games/EUS
