In the fall of 1999, a new game for Linux will be released by Loki: the classic solitaire card game suite Eric's Ultimate Solitaire from Delta Tao Software. With Eric's Ultimate Solitaire, you can enjoy a myriad selection of traditional and not-so-traditional games of solitaire. Choose from 23 different games, track your successes and failures, hone your thinking and strategy skills. Best of all, this version of Eric's Ultimate Solitaire CD is made for Linux operating systems -- a sure favorite for Linux users and cardplayers everywhere.
Eric's Ultimate Software CD for Linux is much more than a deck of cards for your computer. It includes 23 of the most challenging and inventive solitaire games ever devised. These games aren't just luck, either -- they require planning and strategy to win. Some are easy enough that you'll win almost every time, while others seem nearly impossible. All are great fun -- for experts and novices.
