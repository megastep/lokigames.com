By day, you're a researcher on Jupiter's moon Europa. In your free time you re-program the rovers to race around the hallways, battle it out with mini lasers and rocket launchers, and find their way through mazes. It's all in a day's work. Experience the future of strategy and puzzle gaming with CogniToy's MindRover for Linux! Overcome obstacles and navigate treacherous mazes by programming your robots -- and letting them run loose! Without direct control of your robots, you'll have to make sure that your planning is thorough and precise.
