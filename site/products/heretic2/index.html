Minimum System Requirements
Linux Kernel 2.2.x and glibc-2.1
Processor Pentium 166 MHz (with 3D acceleator card) or
Pentium 233 MHz (for software rendering)
Video Video card capable of 800x600 resolution; XFree86 version 3.3.x or newer; 16-bit color
CD-ROM 4x CD-ROM drive (600 KB/s sustained transfer rate)
RAM 32 MB required; 64 MB recommended
Sound OSS compatible sound card
Hard disk Minimum 260 MB free space
